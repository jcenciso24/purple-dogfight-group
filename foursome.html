📖 House Rules

  • 1. No Mulligans: One "breakfast ball" (free shot) on the first tee only.
  • 2. Gimmies should be "inside the putter grip" to prevent constant haggling.
  • 3. No Gimmes for Birdie: All birdie putts must be holed, even if they are short.
  • 4. In winter or wet conditions, allow "lift, clean, and place" within one scorecard length in your own fairway only.
  • 5. Agree on free relief from roots or rocks to protect clubs/prevent injury, but not when in a bunker or hazard.
  • 6. Treat OB as a lateral hazard (one-stroke penalty and drop at the edge) rather than stroke-and-distance, which speeds up play.
  • 7. All money must be collected before the first tee shot is hit.
  • 8. Clearly define payouts: 1st, 2nd, and 3rd place are based off Dogfight Points (DF-Points).
💜 Play well, have fun, and good luck!
